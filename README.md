# Tamamdır – A Peer-to-Peer Local Service Platform for IYTE Gulbahçe Campus

## Project Overview
Tamamdır is a mobile/web-based peer-to-peer local service platform designed for the IYTE Gulbahçe campus community.  
The platform connects students who offer informal services or handmade products with other students who are looking for those services nearby.

Examples of services include:
- tutoring
- tennis coaching
- nail care
- knitting
- handmade goods

The aim of the project is to increase both social interaction and local income opportunities within the campus community.
